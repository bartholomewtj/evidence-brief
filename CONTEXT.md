## Standing rules

- Every statistic is checked against the cited papers. Do not invent.
- Factory (`adws/`, `specs/`, …) stays gitignored.
- **STATE.md tidy:** rewrite the whole file at session end; never append.
  Only *now* and at most three *next* actions. Missing is fine.
